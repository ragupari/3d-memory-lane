# Memory Forest — visual direction

A full-screen spatial keepsake for friends, families, and photographers. The forest is the interface: foreground photos, layered trees, overlapping canopies, a muted earth path, haze, and warm strings of light create parallax as the camera moves.

- Greenfield cinematic, warm humanist design. Variance 7/10, motion 6/10 (slow, controlled), density 2/10, asset dependence 8/10, brand fidelity 5/10.
- Narrative: invitation → personal discovery → quiet reflection.
- Viewing distance: laptop and handheld. Readable controls at screen edges; generous center.
- Palette: forest `#17271e`, cream `#f4efdf`, muted sage `#c4c8b9`, amber `#dcc28d`.
- Locally bundled Cormorant Garamond for headings; Inter for UI.
- Spacing: 8px base, 24–48px screen margins. Small 3–4px corners for paper and dialogs, circular icon controls.
- Shallow paper shadows, background vignette, soft fog. No bright panel backgrounds over the forest except the physical photo inspection sheet.
- Smooth damped camera travel, independent restrained print sway, 200ms controls, 700ms entry. Reduced motion removes sway and particles, shortens transitions, and keeps walking manual by default.

The user supplied a detailed art direction but no attached reference image. The implementation uses that written direction and locally downloaded sample photography with fictional captions.
